# 01 — Case Background and Context

## Capital One and its business context

Capital One is a US financial services company whose products include credit cards, deposits and lending. The case study is relevant to cloud computing because digital banking depends on reliable applications, fast delivery of software changes, and the ability to process customer interactions consistently. Technology is therefore not merely supporting infrastructure; it is part of the customer-facing product and operating model.

In a November 2016 announcement, Amazon stated that Capital One had selected AWS as its predominant cloud infrastructure provider and planned to migrate core business and customer applications over the following years [2]. Capital One’s engineering article published in 2023 describes the cloud decision as beginning in 2014, notes that testing and development environments were in AWS two years later, and says the last data center was closed in 2021 [3]. AWS’s customer case study describes the exit from eight on-premises data centers as completed and associates the journey with an eight-year transformation [1]. These sources use different milestones and dates; the report therefore distinguishes the public 2016 announcement from earlier planning and later completion milestones rather than treating the timeline as a single unambiguous date.

## Why the case matters

The migration illustrates that an enterprise cloud transition is both technical and organizational. Capital One’s published case study describes investment in cloud skills, more agile development, DevOps team ownership, and the use of operational readiness reviews in addition to infrastructure changes [1]. The lesson is that simply copying virtual machines to a cloud provider does not automatically deliver faster releases or better reliability. Teams must also adapt how they design, deploy, observe and operate software.

## Evidence standard

Most numerical outcomes in this report come from AWS’s customer case study [1]. They are presented as reported case-study outcomes, not independently audited measurements from our project. Capital One’s engineering article [3] provides first-party technical context for the documented API pattern. General design recommendations are separately attributed to AWS guidance [4–11].
