## Source-quality note

Sources [1] and [5] are AWS-published customer case studies, so metrics from them are presented as reported case-study outcomes rather than as independently audited results. Sources [2] and [3], [4], [6] provide Amazon/Capital One's own historical or engineering descriptions. The reviewed sources do not provide a clear total dollar cost for Capital One's entire migration.
